🚀 Power BI Project | Campfly Sales Analytics Dashboard 📊

I’m excited to share my latest Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard built to turn sales data into clear, actionable insights.

🔍 What the dashboard covers:
• 💰 Total Revenue & Sales Performance
• 🛒 Total Orders & Units Sold
• 📈 Revenue Trends Over Time
• 🌍 Revenue by Currency
• 📦 Top Products by Revenue
• 🏢 Warehouse Performance
• 📊 Channel-wise Sales Analysis
• 🎛️ Interactive filters for deeper analysis

🛠️ Tools & Skills:
Power BI | Data Visualization | Data Analysis | DAX | Interactive Reporting

The goal was simple: transform raw sales data into an interactive business story that makes performance easier to understand and decisions easier to make. Power BI reports support interactive visuals, filtering, and multi-page analysis, which I used to make the dashboard more exploration-friendly.
